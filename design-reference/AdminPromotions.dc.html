Rules & permissions

Who can change pricing, and how discounts combine.

Combining discounts

Never combined. Each booking gets the single best eligible discount — public offer, private offer, personal discount or approved request. Personal and approved discounts are locked after one use.

How limits are enforced

  1. Checked in the customer’s browser as they type, with a general message.
  2. Re-checked by the server when the request is submitted. Tampered or oversized requests are refused (422).
  3. Re-checked when you decide: the approval can’t exceed what was asked for or your current maximum, and only owners can decide (403).
