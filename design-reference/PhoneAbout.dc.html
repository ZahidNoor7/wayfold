About me

I’m Leyla — and this is the Land of Fire.

I grew up inside the walls of Icherisheher, a few lanes from the Maiden Tower, the daughter of a carpet restorer and a literature teacher. I studied art history at Baku State University and wrote my thesis on the stained glass of Sheki.

In 2014 I guided my first guests — two architects from Copenhagen — and never looked back. Today I lead around ninety journeys a year: still small, still personal, and always with my mother’s shekerbura on day one.
