Overview

Three unhurried days along the old caravan road into the foothills of the Greater Caucasus. We stop at the 10th-century Juma Mosque of Shamakhi, climb to the Diri Baba mausoleum carved into the cliff, and arrive in Sheki in time for sunset from the fortress walls.

You’ll sleep inside the 18th-century Upper Caravanserai, watch master craftsmen assemble shebeke stained glass without a single nail or drop of glue, and return through the cobbled copper-smith village of Lahij.
